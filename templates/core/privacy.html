Privacy notice

Draft for development only. Before real data is used, the operator must confirm retention and hosting arrangements and have this notice reviewed for applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements.

Information held

The system stores account and login information, employee contact and job details, salary and payroll records, attendance and clock times, expenses and uploaded receipts, activity audit records, and failed login attempts including usernames, IP addresses, timestamps, and browser information. Receipts and free-text notes may contain additional personal information entered by users.

How information is used and accessed

This information supports account access, attendance, expense review, stock and payroll administration, and accountability. Access depends on assigned permissions: expense submitters can view their own expenses, while authorized reviewers can view others. Authorized HR and payroll roles can access employee records and payroll information as needed for those workflows.

Cookies and external services

The system uses session and CSRF cookies for sign-in and security. Pages load Bootstrap styling from a third-party content delivery network, which receives requests from your browser. The operator must disclose any hosting, storage, analytics, or other providers actually used before deployment.

Retention and requests

Uploaded receipts are not automatically deleted. A retention and secure-deletion schedule for receipts, employee information, payroll records, logs, and backups has not yet been set. The operator must establish one taking applicable legal obligations into account.

For privacy requests, contact torqueverseke@gmail.com. Requests should be handled in accordance with applicable Kenyan data-protection law.
